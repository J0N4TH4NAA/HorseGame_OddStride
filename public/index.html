HOW TO PLAY

Draw, then cheer.

Draw a creature on the canvas. Start the race. Tap GIDDY UP repeatedly with a steady rhythm. Strong, consistent taps produce speed while poor timing creates fatigue.

Your race horse uses the transparent part of your drawing, so the white drawing paper never appears on the track.

In a friend room, up to four people can join. Each person uses their own device and their own GIDDY UP button. The race starts for everyone at the same time.
